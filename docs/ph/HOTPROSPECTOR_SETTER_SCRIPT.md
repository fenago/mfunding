# Setter Call Scripts — UCC & Aged Leads

> ⚠️ **DRAFT — compliance review required before live dialing.** MCA compliance is baked in
> (advance/funding, not "loan"; no upfront fees; no guarantees; honor opt-out), but per policy
> all merchant-facing copy gets a compliance pass before it goes on the floor.

**Golden rules on every call:** identify yourself + Momentum Funding · it's an *advance / working capital*, never a "loan" · no guarantees of approval/rate/amount · no upfront fees · honor DNC instantly · call 8am–9pm the merchant's local time.

---

## A) UCC LEADS — the poaching pitch
*(These merchants already have an MCA/advance on file — from a UCC filing. Angle: better terms, more capital, or consolidation. Warm-ish.)*

**Opener**
> "Hi, is this the owner of {business}? … Hey {first name}, this is {your name} with **Momentum Funding**. Quick reason for the call — we work with business owners who already have **business funding** in place and are usually leaving money on the table. Do you have 30 seconds?"

**Hook / value**
> "A lot of owners we talk to are carrying **advances at rough factor rates or stacked daily payments**. We help with two things: getting you **additional working capital**, or **consolidating what you've got into one lower payment** so your cash flow breathes. Depending on your numbers we can usually beat what you're in."

**Qualify (quick — don't interrogate)**
- "Roughly what are you doing in monthly revenue?"
- "How long have you been in business?"
- "How many advances/positions are you carrying right now?"
- "If we could free up cash flow or get you more capital, is that something you'd want to look at this week?"

**Set the appointment / transfer**
> "Perfect — I'm going to get you with one of our funding specialists who'll walk you through the options and exact numbers. I can connect you **right now** for two minutes, or lock in {today/tomorrow} at {time} — which is easier?"
> *(Live interest → TRANSFER. Otherwise → BOOK the appointment.)*

## B) AGED LEADS — re-engagement
*(Older/colder leads who once inquired about funding. Angle: check back in, new programs.)*

**Opener**
> "Hi {first name}, {your name} with **Momentum Funding** — you'd looked into **business funding** with us a while back. I'm circling back because our programs have opened up and I wanted to see where you're at. Still running {business}?"

**Hook**
> "We've got **new working-capital programs** with better terms than earlier this year, and approvals are fast — usually 24–48 hours with a few months of bank statements, no upfront cost to you. Worth a quick look?"

**Qualify + set** — same qualifying questions as (A), then book or transfer.

## Objection handling
- **"Not interested."** → "Totally fair — most owners say that until they see the numbers. If I could free up cash flow or get you more capital, would 5 minutes be worth it? … No problem, I'll follow up down the road." *(Respect it — don't push into harassment; DNC if they say stop.)*
- **"I already have funding."** → "That's exactly who we help — most of our clients already had an advance and we either got them more or consolidated it cheaper. What are you paying now?"
- **"Just send me info."** → "Happy to — what's the best email? And let's grab 10 minutes so I send the *right* options, not generic stuff. {book}."
- **"How'd you get my number?"** → "You're on public business filings / you'd inquired about funding with us. If you'd rather not hear from us, I'll take you off the list right now." *(If yes → DNC.)*
- **"I'm busy."** → "I'll be quick or I'll call back — what's better, later today or tomorrow morning?" *(book a callback.)*
- **"Take me off your list."** → "Done — I'm removing you now. Have a good one." *(Mark DNC immediately. No re-dial.)*

## Voicemail
> "Hi {first name}, {your name} with **Momentum Funding** — reaching out about **working capital** options for {business}. Give me a call back at {number}, or I'll try you again. Thanks!"
*(Keep it short; leave the callback number clearly.)*

## What NOT to say
- ❌ "loan" (for MCA products) — say advance / funding / working capital.
- ❌ "You're approved" / "guaranteed" / a specific rate or amount before underwriting.
- ❌ Anything implying an upfront fee.
- ❌ Arguing after a clear "stop / do not call."
