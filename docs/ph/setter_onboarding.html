Your first day on the dialer

You call business owners who already have funding, get them interested, and hand them to a funding specialist — capturing what you learn in MFunding so we can send their application while they're still on the phone. This is everything you need for day one: two logins, how to start dialing, and exactly what to do when someone answers.

01

Set up your two logins (once)

The dialer — where you call
HotProspector
app.hotprospector.com
Your email + the password you set from the setup link your manager sends you.
The cockpit — where the merchant opens
MFunding
mfunding.net
Same email & password. Stay logged in here in the same browser — it's where a merchant's Playbook opens when you're on a call.
Setting your password
The setup link your manager sends opens a page to choose your own password — it's single-use and expires in about an hour, so set it right when you get it. Forgot it later? Go to mfunding.net → “Forgot your password?”
02

Start dialing

  1. Log into HotProspector
    Open app.hotprospector.com and sign in.
  2. Open your campaign
    Left menu → Dialer. Open the campaign assigned to you: “UCC Parallel-3”.
  3. Press the green ▶ and start the session
    A Session Settings box appears → tick your time zone → Start Dialing.
  4. It dials 3 at once 3-line
    The dialer rings three numbers at the same time and connects you the instant one person answers — the other two drop. You talk; it lines up the next set when you hang up.
03

When someone answers

  1. Read the script
    It auto-loads on the Script tab of your call screen. Follow it — it keeps you compliant. Remember: it's an advance / working capital, a loan.
  2. Open the merchant in MFunding
    On the contact card, look at the bottom of the right-hand sidebar and click “Gohighlevel Custom Link” (it has a ↗ icon). It opens the Revenue Playbook with this merchant already loaded.
  3. Work the Playbook while you have them
    Fill in the merchant's details, send the application, and get them to connect their bank / upload statements right there on the call. “Let's knock this out while I've got you.”
  4. Disposition the call
    Pick the outcome so the right follow-up fires: Send Application, Call Back, Not Interested, etc. If they say “take me off your list,” mark DNC immediately.
One thing not to click
There's a tab called “Playbook” on the HotProspector call screen — that is not ours. The button that opens MFunding is “Gohighlevel Custom Link” on the contact card. Use that one.
04

Say it right — every call

  • ✕
    It's an advance, working capital, or funding — never “a loan.”
  • ✓
    Open with the recording notice: “this call may be recorded.”
  • ✓
    No upfront fees. No guarantees of approval, rate, or amount — it's subject to underwriting.
  • ✓
    Call only 8am–9pm the merchant's local time (the dialer already enforces this — don't override it).
  • ✕
    “Take me off your list” → mark DNC on the spot. No arguing, no re-dial.
05

If something's off

The MFunding button shows a login screen, not the merchant
You're not signed into mfunding.net. Log in there in the same browser, then click “Gohighlevel Custom Link” again.
“Out of leads” / nothing dials
Tell your manager — your campaign needs more leads loaded, or you're not assigned yet.
Can't log in
Use “Forgot your password?” on mfunding.net, or ask your manager to resend your setup link.
Someone's angry you called
Stay calm, apologize, and if they say stop — DNC them and end the call. Never argue.
