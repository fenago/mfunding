How it works

1
Pick the shape, not just the amount
A term loan is a fixed sum with a fixed end date. A line of credit sits there until you draw on it and you pay for what you use. They suit different problems.
2
Send a real file
Signed application and four months of statements. Over $250,000, a year-to-date profit and loss and a balance sheet as well.
3
Underwriting takes its time
This is real credit, so a person reads it. A term loan or a line moves fastest; equipment takes longer; SBA is measured in weeks rather than days, and we will say so on day one rather than at the end.
4
Terms in writing, then you decide
Amount, rate, term, fees and payment, in a document, before anything is signed.
